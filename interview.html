About the Archive

The Liberation War of 1971 defines the birth of our nation. This digital archive serves as a permanent guardian of that history.

Our mission is to collect, digitize, and display the authentic records of the war. From the Proclamation of Independence to the personal diaries of Freedom Fighters, we ensure these stories are never lost.
